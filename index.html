Five rules I work by.

  1. 01

    Make progress you can measure

    Every system, report, or automation has to move the business forward in a way I can measure and defend.

  2. 02

    Give more in value than you receive in compensation

    Deliver clear answers early, raise problems before they get expensive, and work for the whole company.

  3. 03

    Everyone sweeps the floor

    No task is beneath the person accountable for the result.

  4. 04

    Share good news quickly, and bad news even quicker

    The sooner the people who can act hear about a problem, the smaller the fix.

  5. 05

    Treat every experience as a chance to learn

    When something goes wrong, ask what we saw too late and how to see it sooner next time.
